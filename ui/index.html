How to use this TV schedule — instructions

1. Find a broadcast. Choose a date or search by programme, team or channel. Times shown are Melbourne broadcast times, not necessarily the actual start or finish of the sporting event. The venue's operating hours determine which programmes appear.

2. Check clashes. When two broadcast time slots overlap, you'll see Clash detected. Select its i button to view the other programmes and their broadcast times. Broadcasts that meet exactly at their end/start boundary are not a clash.

3. Choose a preferred screening. Managers can turn on Prefer for the event they'd rather screen. Conflicting alternatives are dimmed, but remain in the schedule and PDF as backups for cancellations, rain delays or changes. Use Clear preferred screenings to reset this venue's priorities without changing PDF exclusions.

4. Exclude a programme from the PDF. Managers can switch on Omit from PDF for an individual broadcast. It becomes dimmed in the dashboard and is excluded from the export, but remains available to restore. This differs from Omit title, which removes all programmes with that title from the venue schedules and is available only to the administrator.

5. Export the schedule. Select Export TV schedule (PDF). In the print window choose Save as PDF. The export covers all upcoming programmes eligible for this venue, even when the current search or date filter is narrower. Non-preferred alternatives appear grey; programmes omitted from the PDF do not appear.

Saving choices: Screening priorities and PDF exclusions currently save only in this browser. They are not automatically shared with other managers or devices. This is a planning guide; always confirm live broadcast arrangements with the broadcaster.
